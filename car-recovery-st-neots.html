Covering St Neots and its busy junctions

St Neots has two major trunk roads on its doorstep. Here's what to do and where we help.

The A1 & the Black Cat

The Black Cat roundabout, where the A1 meets the A421 just south of St Neots, is one of the busiest junctions in the area. If you stop nearby, get well away from the traffic and call 999 if you're in danger. Once you're safe, tell us which road and which direction you were heading.

The A428 towards Caxton Gibbet

The A428 runs east from St Neots towards Caxton Gibbet and Cambridge. Give us the direction you're travelling and the nearest junction or landmark, and we'll tell you straight away whether we can help.

St Neots station & Love's Farm

Commuter cars sitting all day in the cold are prime candidates for a flat battery. Call from the car park and we'll come out with the ECU-protected jump kit.

Eaton Socon, Eynesbury & Little Paxton

Car recovery in St Neots: FAQs

You're in Bedfordshire. Do you really come out to St Neots?

Yes. St Neots is a short run up the A1 from our base in Sandy, and it's part of our normal local recovery area. Call 07866 000888 any time, day or night.

I've broken down near the Black Cat or on the A428. What should I do?

Get yourself and any passengers safe first: hazard lights on, out on the side away from the traffic, and behind the barrier if there is one. If you're in a live lane or in danger, call 999 first. Once you're safe, call us with the road, your direction of travel and the nearest junction, and we'll tell you straight away whether we can help.

My car won't start at St Neots station. Can you jump start it?

Yes. We come out to the station car park, Love's Farm and anywhere else in town with an ECU-protected jump kit that protects your car's electronics. If it turns out to be more than the battery, we can recover the car home or to a garage.

Do you cover Eaton Socon, Eynesbury and Little Paxton?

Yes. We cover all of St Neots, including Eaton Socon, Eaton Ford, Eynesbury, Love's Farm, Little Paxton and Great Paxton, plus Wyboston, Abbotsley, Croxton and Buckden.
