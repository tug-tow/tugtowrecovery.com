Free collection FAQs

What documents do I need?

Please have the vehicle's V5C logbook ready when we collect, along with any keys you have. If you can't find the logbook, call us on 07866 000888 and we'll talk you through it.

Does the vehicle need to run?

No. Non-runners, failed MOTs, flat tyres and vehicles that haven't moved in years are all fine. We'll winch it onto the truck.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Sandy and collect across Bedfordshire, including Sandy, Bedford, Biggleswade, Potton and Shefford, plus St Neots, Huntingdon, Milton Keynes and nearby areas. If you're further afield, give us a call and ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no charge for collecting and disposing of your unwanted vehicle. Just call 07866 000888 to book a collection.
