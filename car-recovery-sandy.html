Straight answers

Car recovery in Sandy: FAQs

Are you actually based in Sandy?

Yes. Tug & Tow Recovery has been based in Sandy, Bedfordshire, since 2002, a few minutes from the A1. When you call 07866 000888 you speak to Scott directly, not a call centre.

I've broken down on the A1 near Sandy. What should I do?

Get yourself and any passengers safe first. Pull onto the verge or hard strip if you can, put your hazard lights on, get out on the side away from the traffic and wait behind the barrier if there is one. If you're stopped in a live lane or in danger, call 999 first.

Once you're safe, call us on 07866 000888 and tell us which carriageway you're on (northbound or southbound) and the nearest junction or landmark. We'll tell you straight away whether we can help and give you a quote.

Can you collect a car I've bought from Copart Sandy?

Yes. We collect vehicles from Copart Sandy, including damaged cars and non-runners, and deliver them wherever you need them. Call 07866 000888 for a quote.

My car failed its MOT at a Sandy garage. Can you take it away?

Yes. If it isn't worth repairing, we'll collect it free of charge from the garage or your home and make sure it's recycled properly. If it still has some value, call us for an offer instead. See free vehicle collection and we buy any vehicle.

Do you cover Potton, Everton and Moggerhanger too?

Yes. Around Sandy we cover Potton, Everton, Moggerhanger, Blunham, Beeston, Girtford, Tempsford, Northill and Biggleswade, plus Bedford and St Neots. If you're not sure, just ring and ask.
