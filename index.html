Free advice

Check your car before a journey

A few minutes of checks before a long drive can save you a breakdown, and some of them are a legal requirement.

Tyre pressures

Check pressures when the tyres are cold, before you set off. The correct figures are in your handbook or on the sticker inside the fuel flap or door frame. Don't forget the spare, and use the higher setting if you're carrying a full load.

Oil level

Park on level ground with the engine off and give it a few minutes to settle. Pull out the dipstick, wipe it, push it fully back in and pull it out again. The oil should sit between the min and max marks. Top up with the oil your handbook recommends, a little at a time, and don't overfill.

Tyre tread

The UK legal minimum is 1.6mm across the central three-quarters of the tread, all the way around the tyre. For a quick guide, use the 20p test: put a 20p coin into the main grooves. If you can see the outer band of the coin, the tread may be too low, so have it checked by a tyre fitter.

Keep it clean & visible

Keep your windows, mirrors, lights and number plates clean so you can see and be seen. The law requires your number plates and lights to be visible, and a dirty or unreadable plate can lead to a fine. Check all your lights work, and top up the screenwash.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I break down?

Get yourself and any passengers safe first. Pull over as far as you can, put your hazard lights on and, if it's safe, wait away from the traffic (behind a barrier if there is one). If you're in a dangerous position on a live carriageway, call 999 first.

Then call us on 07866 000888 and tell us where you are (postcode, road name or what3words), the make and model of the vehicle and what's happened. We'll tell you what's involved and give you a quote before we set off.

Do you cover motorways and fast roads?

Your safety comes first. If you've stopped on a motorway or fast dual carriageway, get out on the side away from the traffic, wait behind the barrier if it's safe, and call 999 if you're in danger. Once you're safe, call us and tell us exactly where you are, and we'll tell you straight away whether we can help.

How much does recovery cost?

Every job is different, so we don't list prices. Call 07866 000888 for a quote and we'll agree it with you before we start.

What vehicles can you move?

We tow cars and vans, and also move motorbikes and light commercial vehicles. Our compact recovery truck also suits tight spaces and low cars. We don't move HGVs, buses or heavy plant.

Can you deliver a car I've bought?

Yes. We collect new and used cars from dealers, auctions (including Copart Sandy) and private sellers and deliver them to your home anywhere in the UK. We can also take your car to a dealer or garage for you.

Do you do van recovery as well as cars?

Yes. We recover and tow cars and vans, including light commercials, 24/7 across Sandy, Bedford, Biggleswade and the rest of Bedfordshire. Call 07866 000888 for a quote.

I've locked my keys in the car. Can you help?

Yes. We can open the car using non-destructive entry methods and get your keys back to you. Every car is different, so we'll explain what's involved before we start.

My car has gone off the road or overturned. Can you recover it?

Yes. We recover vehicles stuck in mud or driven off the road into a field, ditch or bushes, and we right and recover overturned cars and vans. If anyone is hurt or trapped, or the vehicle is blocking a road, call 999 first.

Where are you based and how far do you go?

We're based in Sandy, Bedfordshire. For towing, breakdowns and local recovery we cover Sandy, Bedford, Biggleswade, St Neots, Potton, Shefford, Huntingdon, Milton Keynes and across Bedfordshire. For vehicle delivery we go anywhere in the UK.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. We're open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and bank holidays. Phoning is always the quickest way to reach us.
