# Image credits – Tug & Tow Recovery website preview

Photos updated 15 Oct 2026. The home-page hero is Scott's own photo, used with his approval (15 Oct 2026). Every other photo is free-licence stock (Unsplash License or Pexels License). They were downloaded to the box and are served locally from `assets/img/`. Each was resized to 1600px or 1200px wide and saved as WebP plus a JPEG fallback, with every file under 400KB.

**Plates and branding.** Every readable number plate is blurred and pixelated. Company names, logos, phone numbers and web addresses are blurred or painted out. The unedited originals are kept outside the site folder, in `/workspace/website-image-originals/`, so they can never be uploaded by mistake. The edits are applied by `_src/process_images.py`, so they can be reproduced.
